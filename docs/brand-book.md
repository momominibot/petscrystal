## 1. Brand Foundation

### Brand Essence
> *A quiet tether between two souls — one human, one animal — woven through stone and intention.*

Pets Crystal exists at the intersection of spiritual wellness and modern pet parenthood. We create matching crystal jewelry for humans and their animals: pieces that carry the same stone, the same energy, the same intention, worn by two beings who belong to each other.

**We are not a pet accessory brand.** We are a connection brand. The jewelry is the tangible expression of something invisible — the bond, the energy, the unspoken understanding between a person and their animal.

## 2. Brand Voice Guidelines

### Voice Pillars

#### ✦ Warm
We speak like someone who just poured you tea. Our voice is soft, unhurried, and genuinely kind. We use inclusive language. We never talk down or use jargon as armor.

**Sounds like:** "We chose rose quartz for this set because it's the stone of unconditional love — and if anyone deserves that reminder, it's the dog who greets you like you've been gone for years every time you walk through the door."

**Does not sound like:** "Our proprietary rose quartz collection leverages premium gemstone sourcing for optimal aesthetic outcomes."

#### ✦ Knowing
We understand crystals, but we share that knowledge like a friend, not a professor. We explain, we don't lecture. We connect the stone's properties to real emotional experiences.

**Sounds like:** "Amethyst has been used for centuries as a calming stone. We like to think of it as a deep breath in crystal form — for the human who overthinks, and the anxious rescue who needs a little extra peace."

#### ✦ Intimate
We speak one-to-one. Every piece of copy should feel like it was written for the person reading it. We use "you" generously. We acknowledge the specificity of loving one particular animal.

**Sounds like:** "This bracelet is for you. The matching collar charm is for the creature who sleeps on your pillow, steals your spot on the couch, and somehow makes every day better just by existing."

#### ✦ Joyful (Quietly)
Not loud. Not performative. The humor of someone who has found their cat sleeping in the bathroom sink for the fourth time this week and just... accepts it. Gentle, knowing smiles. Recognition humor for pet people.

**Sounds like:** "Black tourmaline for protection. Because your cat definitely saw something in the corner at 3am, and honestly, so did you."

### Tone Spectrum

| Context | Tone Dial | Example Trigger |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| Product descriptions | Reverent, sensory | New collection launch |
| Educational content | Warm, clear, unhurried | Crystal properties explainer |
| Social captions | Intimate, conversational, sometimes playful | Instagram carousel |
| Email marketing | Personal, generous, slightly more direct | Welcome sequence |
| Customer service | Empathetic, solution-oriented, human | Sizing help |
| About / Brand story | Lyrical, grounded, sincere | About page |

### Language Guidelines

**Words we love:**
connection · intention · gentle · ritual · bond · companion · soft · quiet · grounded · warmth · presence · reminder · belonging · trust · steady · luminous · attentive · sacred · tender · soul

**Words we avoid:**
pet parent (overused) · pawrent (cutesy) · furbaby (infantilizing) · obsessed · must-have · trendy · luxury (as a standalone adjective — earn it, don't declare it) · bestie · obsessed · literally · game-changer · obsessed · iconic · slay

**Grammar & Style:**
- Sentence fragments are welcome when they create rhythm
- Em dashes — not hyphens — for asides
- Oxford comma: yes
- Ampersands: only in titles/headings, never in body
- Exclamation points: sparingly. One per page maximum. Let the words carry the feeling
- Contractions: encouraged. "We're" not "we are"
- The diamond motif ✦ can be used as a section divider or decorative element, never mid-sentence

### Message Hierarchy

```
1. THE BOND — "You and your animal share something real.
   We make it visible."

2. THE STONE — "Every crystal carries intention.
   Here's what this one means for you both."

3. THE PIECE — "Thoughtfully designed. Made to last.
   Worn together."

4. THE BRAND — "Pets Crystal. Matching jewelry for
   connected souls."
```

Always lead with connection. Never lead with product. The jewelry is the vehicle; the bond is the destination.

## 3. Visual Direction

### Color System

| Color | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| Warm Cream | `#FBF6EE` | Backgrounds, negative space, "breathing room" |
| Ink | `#2A211B` | Primary text, grounding elements, deep contrast |
| Lavender | `#C4B5D4` | Accent, secondary CTAs, soft energy cues |
| Rose | `#E8D5D0` | Warmth accents, blush overlays, emotional warmth |
| Gold | `#D4A84B` | ✦ motif, highlights, premium cues, jewelry hardware |

### Typography

**Headings:** Fraunces (serif) — warm, editorial, slightly old-world but modern in weight. Use in:
- H1 page titles
- Product names
- Quote pull-outs
- Blog post titles

**Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans — clean, approachable, highly readable. Use in:
- All body copy
- Navigation
- Product descriptions
- Captions
- UI elements

**Usage rules:**
- Fraunces at display sizes (48px+) in Light (300) or Regular (400) weight
- Fraunces at heading sizes (24-36px) in SemiBold (600)
- Jakarta Sans body at 16-18px, Regular (400)
- Never use Fraunces below 18px
- Letter-spacing on Fraunces headings: -0.5% to -1%
- Line height: 1.4 for Fraunces, 1.6 for Jakarta Sans body

### The ✦ Diamond Motif
- Used as a decorative divider between sections
- Appears on packaging, tissue paper, and hang tags
- Never used as a bullet point (that's what it looks like; we're intentional)
- Can be scaled up as a watermark or subtle background pattern
- In gold (`#D4A84B`) or cream (`#FBF6EE`) depending on background

### Imagery Principles

**Overall Direction:**
Editorial meets wellness. Think: *Kinfolk* magazine meets a crystal healing studio meets golden hour in a sun-drenched apartment. Soft, intentional, never sterile. Warm shadows. Real textures. The opposite of Amazon product photography.

**Lighting:**
- Natural light dominant — north-facing window light when possible
- Golden hour warmth for lifestyle shots
- Soft diffused light, never harsh or direct flash
- Shadows are welcome — they create depth and mood
- Avoid: ring lights, clinical white-background studio lighting, heavy post-processing

**Composition:**
- Negative space is generous — let pieces breathe
- Asymmetrical compositions preferred
- Pieces shown in context: on skin, on fur, on surfaces
- Detail shots: the clasp, the stone's natural inclusions, the way light passes through
- Wider shots: the human hand resting on the animal, the matching pieces visible

**Styling Surfaces & Props:**
| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| Surfaces | Raw linen, unglazed ceramic, warm wood, travertine, handmade paper, nubby wool |
| Props | Dried flowers (not fresh — preserved, intentional), open books, ceramic vessels, quartz clusters, incense trails, half-drunk tea |
| Textiles | Linen, gauze, raw silk, knit wool — all in the brand palette |
| Avoid | Plastic, shiny synthetics, neon anything, mirrored surfaces, fake plants |

**Models & Casting:**
- Real people with real animals. Not professional pet models.
- Diverse representation across age (25-55), ethnicity, body type
- Animals should look comfortable, loved, at ease — never posed unnaturally
- Hands are important: shown gently touching the animal, adjusting the collar, holding a paw
- The jewelry should feel like it's already part of their life, not something they put on for a shoot
- Candid over posed. Connection over perfection.

**Animal Representation:**
- Dogs and cats primary, but inclusive of all companion animals
- Animals shown in their natural state: sleeping, being held, walking, looking at their human
- Fur texture embraced — it adds warmth and life to the image
- Never costume, never forced posing, never anything that compromises the animal's comfort

## 4. Product Photography Shot List

### Essential Hero Shots (per product)
1. **The Connection** — Human hand resting on animal, both wearing their pieces. Soft focus background. Editorial composition. *Primary hero image.*
2. **The Stone** — Extreme close-up of the crystal, natural light passing through. Visible inclusions celebrated, not retouched out.
3. **Worn by Human** — On skin, against neutral backdrop (linen, cream, skin tone). Natural posture, not mannequin-like.
4. **Worn by Animal** — Collar charm on fur. Dog or cat in relaxed state. The piece as part of their life, not an accessory they're modeling.
5. **The Pair** — Both pieces together on a warm surface. Morning light. Botanical elements (dried lavender, eucalyptus).
6. **The Detail** — Clasp, setting, chain weight, gold hardware. Craftsmanship visible.
7. **In Context** — Wider lifestyle shot. Human and animal in a beautiful, lived-in space. Candid moment. The jewelry is present but not the focal point.

### Seasonal / Campaign Shots
- Holiday: warm low light, candles, fur and blankets, the ✦ motif in gold foil
- Spring: morning light, open windows, fresh linens, light crystals (rose quartz, clear quartz)
- Fall: deeper shadows, knit textures, amber-toned stones, books and tea
- Everyday: the rhythm of life with an animal — morning coffee with a cat in lap, evening walk with dog, reading with pet asleep nearby

## 7. Brand in Practice

### Social Media Voice Examples

**Good:**
> "She follows you into every room. She knows your moods better than you do. This bracelet + charm set is for the dog who has seen you at your worst and stayed anyway. Rose quartz. Unconditional. ✦"

**Not our voice:**
> "Obsessed with our new rose quartz drop! 😍🐾 Matching with your furbaby has never been cuter! Link in bio!!"

**Good:**
> "Black tourmaline is the stone of protection. We like to think of it as a quiet 'I've got you' — for late-night walks and new places and the moments when you both need to feel a little braver."

**Not our voice:**
> "Protect your energy vibes with our AMAZING new tourmaline collection!!! 🔮✨ Limited stock, get yours before they're gone!!!"

### Packaging Direction
- Unbleached, textured paper boxes in warm cream
- Gold foil ✦ on the lid
- Tissue paper in rose or lavender
- Hand-tied with natural cotton cord
- A small card explaining the crystal's properties, written in the brand voice
- No plastic. No shiny coatings. No excessive packaging.
- The unboxing should feel like opening a gift from someone who knows you

### Email Marketing Voice
- Subject lines: warm and specific, never urgent or salesy
  - **Good:** "Something for you. Something for them."
  - **Not:** "⚡FLASH SALE 24 HOURS ONLY⚡"
- Welcome sequence: 4 emails over 2 weeks
  1. The story — why we started
  2. The stones — what each crystal means
  3. The connection — how to wear them together
  4. The invitation — find your matching stone
