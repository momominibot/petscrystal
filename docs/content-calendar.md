## Notes & Caveats

1. **Site unreachable:** petscrystals.com did not resolve during research (HTTP 000 / timeout). Crystal-stone assignments for product names are based on naming conventions and standard crystal jewelry pairings. Verify actual stone matches against the live product catalog before publishing stone-specific content.

2. **Search volumes:** All volume estimates are approximations based on SERP feature analysis, related keyword data, and competitive landscape — not from a paid keyword tool. Use Ahrefs, Semrush, or Google Keyword Planner for precise validation.

3. **"Healing crystals for pets" and "crystal healing for dogs":** These head terms are competitive (Chewy DA 75+, vet sites, Whole Dog Journal). This calendar targets long-tail angles ("for anxious dogs", "by breed", "by temperament") rather than competing head-on.

4. **Medical disclaimer:** All pet safety and healing content should include clear disclaimers that crystal healing is complementary and does not replace veterinary care.

5. **Internal linking:** Every post should link to relevant product collection pages and to 2–3 other blog posts in the calendar to build topic clusters and distribute page authority.
